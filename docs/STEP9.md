## Earnings and payouts implemented

The DEV-only `/finance-preview` route reads the same persisted sample booking as Steps 6–8. It is linked from the provider Home/account earnings action and completed-work earnings action. Available, pending release, wallet posting, reserved payout and repayment debt are separate. Customer completion and a ledger release do not themselves add spendable labour money: a separately matched, idempotent wallet posting is required. Reports use actual saved release timestamps; undated releases appear in All time. The snapshot has no gross release/component-fee allocation, so the report labels its known gross amount **Gross booking payment**, and preserves the original booking fee rather than fabricating a gross release or charging another fee.

Finance writes share the booking store's serialized command queue and revision checks. Sample bank verification binds provider, approved legal identity, bank, account and lookup reference. The business display name stays separate from the approved legal identity. Bank updates invalidate unconsumed payout approval; already requested payouts keep their frozen destination. Sample authorization binds amount, wallet, currency and destination, is consumed once, and reserves funds atomically. A matched success consumes the reservation; a matched failure releases it. No additional payout fee is assessed.

The fictional reservation fixture explicitly supplies material lots using its known sample balances. This is not a selected production lot policy. Cancellation can recover unreserved net materials immediately. A subsequent failed payout releases its reserved lot and recovers only the still-due amount. A pending hosted repayment prevents that recovery from racing the same obligation; recovery resumes after a matched failed repayment, or the matched successful repayment settles it. Initial snapshots cannot overwrite wallets after postings or reservations.
